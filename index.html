Mind Ladder Privacy Policy

App: Mind Ladder 3D (Android, package com.mindladder.game3d)
Effective date: 10 November 2026

In short
  • Mind Ladder does not ask for your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or location.
  • There are no accounts, no chat and no way to share personal information with other players.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob. They are set up for an audience that includes children: no personalised ads, no advertising ID, and family-friendly ads only.

1. Who we are

Mind Ladder 3D ("Mind Ladder", "the game", "we") is a quiz game made by an independent developer. If you have any question about this policy, contact us at gerald30.munyoro@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. We have no servers that receive information from the game, and we do not use analytics or tracking tools.

3. Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves the following on your device only:

  • your level, experience points and coin total;
  • your best score, number of games played and answer accuracy;
  • which questions you have already seen, so they are not repeated too soon;
  • the categories you picked, whether sound is on, and the date you last claimed the daily bonus.

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the game.

4. Advertising

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, a service provided by Google. Some ads are optional "rewarded" videos you can choose to watch for a bonus, such as extra coins or a second chance. A full-screen ad can also appear between games.

Because the game's audience includes children, every ad request is sent to Google marked as child-directed and under the age of consent. This means:

  • ads are not personalised and are not based on your interests or past behaviour;
  • the game does not use the Android advertising ID;
  • only ads suitable for general audiences (rated G) are shown.

To show an ad, Google's software in the game may still receive limited technical information from your device, such as its IP address, device model, operating system version, language and how you interact with the ad. Google uses this to deliver the ad, limit how often you see it, measure it and prevent fraud. Google handles this information under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • AdMob and child-directed treatment

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game may show a consent message from Google before ads are loaded.

5. Children's privacy

Mind Ladder is suitable for players of all ages, including children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13. The game has no sign-up, no chat and no way for players to share personal details. Ads are set up for children as described in section 4.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child's information has been collected through the game, please contact us at gerald30.munyoro@gmail.com and we will help.

We do not sell, rent or share personal information, because we do not collect any. The only third party that receives information from the game is Google, as described in section 4.

8. Security

Your progress is stored in the game's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the effective date at the top. If a change is significant, we will also mention it in the game's update notes.
