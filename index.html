Mind Ladder Privacy Policy

App: Mind Ladder 3D (Android, package com.mindladder.game3d)
Effective date: 10 November 2026

In short
  • Mind Ladder does not ask for your name, email, phone number, photos, contacts or location.
  • There are no accounts, no chat and no way to share personal information with other players.
  • Your game progress is saved only on your device.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID to show and measure ads.
  • Mind Ladder is meant for players aged 13 and over.

1. Who we are

Mind Ladder 3D ("Mind Ladder", "the game", "we") is a quiz game made by an independent developer. If you have any question about this policy, contact us at gerald30.munyoro@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. We have no servers that receive information from the game, and we do not use analytics or tracking tools.

3. Information stored on your device

To remember your progress, the game saves the following on your device only:

  • your level, experience points and coin total;
  • your best score, number of games played and answer accuracy;
  • which questions you have already seen, so they are not repeated too soon;
  • the categories you picked and whether sound is on.

This information never leaves your device and is not sent to us. You can delete it at any time by clearing the app's data in your device settings or by uninstalling the game.

4. Advertising

The game is free and shows ads from Google AdMob, a service provided by Google. Some ads are optional "rewarded" videos you can choose to watch for a bonus, such as a second chance or double coins. A full-screen ad can also appear between games.

To show ads, Google's software in the game may collect information from your device, such as:

  • your device's advertising ID;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device model, operating system version and language;
  • how you interact with ads.

Google uses this information to show ads (which may be personalised to your interests), limit how often you see an ad, measure ad performance and prevent fraud. Ads are limited to content suitable for teens and older (rated T or lower). Google handles this information under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows a consent message from Google before ads are loaded, where you can choose how your information is used.

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of personalised ads at any time in your device settings (on most Android devices: Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).

5. Children's privacy

Mind Ladder is intended for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. The game has no sign-up, no chat and no way for players to share personal details.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child's information has been collected through the game, please contact us at gerald30.munyoro@gmail.com and we will help.

8. Security

Your progress is stored in the game's private storage on your device, which other apps cannot read.
